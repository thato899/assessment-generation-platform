# CAPS Physical Sciences curriculum slice

Issue #2 represents the Grade 12 Mechanics topics needed by the current backlog. The source is the South African Department of Basic Education's official CAPS Physical Sciences Grades 10–12 document:

- [CAPS Physical Sciences Grades 10–12 (DBE PDF)](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/CD/National%20Curriculum%20Statements%20and%20Vocational/CAPS%20FET%20%20PHYSICAL%20SCIENCE%20WEB.pdf)

The Grade 12 Mechanics entries represented here are Momentum and Impulse, Vertical projectile motion in one dimension (1D), and Work, Energy & Power. The vertical-projectile entry records the source constraints “near the surface of the Earth” and “in the absence of air friction”, plus the source's representation forms: words, diagrams, equations and graphs.

This module is metadata only. It does not calculate physics, generate scenarios, render diagrams, or validate API requests.
