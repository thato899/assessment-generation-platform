# CAPS Physical Sciences curriculum slice

Issue #2 represents the initial Grade 12 Mechanics topics needed by the
backlog. Issue #54 adds the evidence-backed Grade 11 Newton's Laws topic and
records its Grade 12 consolidation and selected-examinability relationship.
The source is the South African Department of Basic Education's official CAPS
Physical Sciences Grades 10–12 document:

- [CAPS Physical Sciences Grades 10–12 (DBE PDF)](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/CD/National%20Curriculum%20Statements%20and%20Vocational/CAPS%20FET%20%20PHYSICAL%20SCIENCE%20WEB.pdf)

The Grade 11 Mechanics entry represented here is Newton's Laws and Application
of Newton's Laws. It is owned by Grade 11 in the metadata because CAPS places
the core instruction there. Grade 12 uses the material for consolidation,
integrated problem solving, and selected final-examination content.

The Grade 12 Mechanics entries are Momentum and Impulse, Vertical projectile
motion in one dimension (1D), and Work, Energy & Power. The vertical-projectile
entry records the source constraints “near the surface of the Earth” and “in
the absence of air friction”, plus the source's representation forms: words,
diagrams, equations and graphs.

This module is metadata only. It does not calculate physics, generate
scenarios, render diagrams, or validate API requests.
